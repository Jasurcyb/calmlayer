Medical discharge instructions

Normal webDense instructions

Following discharge, the patient should maintain adequate oral hydration and adhere to the prescribed pharmacological regimen, including administration at the indicated intervals, while avoiding strenuous physical exertion and monitoring for clinically significant exacerbation of symptoms, including but not limited to persistent dyspnea, syncope, uncontrolled pain, fever exceeding 38°C, altered mental status, or evidence of infection. In the event of deterioration, the patient should promptly contact the treating physician or seek emergency evaluation.

What to do now

Take your medicine exactly as instructed. Drink water and rest today.

What to avoid

Avoid heavy exercise until your care team says it is safe.

When to get help

Get urgent medical help for severe breathing trouble, fainting, confusion, uncontrolled pain, or a high fever.
